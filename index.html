The House of SEDA

Where Heritage
Becomes Heirloom

Kosa silk — the 'Queen of Silks' — is spun from the cocoons of the Antheraea mylitta silkworm, feeding only on siali and arjun trees. In Bilaspur, our weavers take 15–20 days to complete a single saree on pit looms, using natural dyes and zari work passed down through generations.

About SEDA

Elegance, Heritage,
Timeless Luxury

Born in Bilaspur, Worn with Pride

SEDA was founded on a simple belief: true luxury is made by hand, slowly and honestly. We work directly with 40+ weaver families in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — no middlemen, fair wages, and a share of every saree funding weaver children's education.

Kosa silk has been woven in this region for over 2,000 years, mentioned in ancient texts and favoured by maharanis. Today, SEDA brings that legacy to modern wardrobes — festive, wedding and heirloom sarees that outlive trends.
